Insurance & coverage

Psychological services are not covered by OHIP. However, many extended health benefits plans provide full or partial coverage for psychological services. Please contact your insurance provider to determine your level of coverage.

No referral is needed. You can contact me directly if you believe you or your child could benefit from therapy.

Practical

Before you book

01

Where you can be

Clients can be located in Ontario, Quebec or Nova Scotia. Sessions are remote only — there is currently no in-person option.

02

What you'll need

A private space and a stable internet connection. Many clients find it easier to open up from somewhere they already feel at ease.

03

Which language

Sessions can be held in English or Spanish, whichever helps you feel most at home.

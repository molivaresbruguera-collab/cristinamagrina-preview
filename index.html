Meet Cristina

Extensive clinical training and experience, delivered with warmth

Starting in Barcelona, Spain and continuing my career in Toronto, Canada - throughout the years I have supported children, adolescents, adults, and families through times of change, difficulty, and growth.

I take a warm and collaborative approach, tailoring therapy to each person and combining a practical and human perspective. To me, therapy begins with feeling safe, heard, and understood, and ends with tools that help you move forward through every stage of life.

Approach

A thoughtful approach that works

Cristina's extensive experience allows her to blend clinical training with a warm, collaborative style to help every individual thrive.

My work is practical and grounded in evidence-based practice. I draw from Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Dialectical Behavioural Therapy (DBT), and Solution-Focused Brief Therapy (SFBT)/coaching — integrating them in a way that fits your goals, strengths, and preferences.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Use the booking button to schedule a free 15-minute consultation. This time will help me learn more about what brings you to therapy and what you hope to get out of it, as well as answer any questions you may have. We will also talk about what therapy would look like and next steps to follow.

Do you offer sessions in person or online?

I am currently available to provide psychological services remotely via secure video call. Clients can be in Ontario, Quebec or Nova Scotia and focus on doing the meaningful psychotherapy work they deserve from the comfort of their home or another private space.

Are services covered by OHIP?

No, psychological services are not covered by OHIP. However, many private insurance plans provide full or partial coverage for psychological services. Please contact your insurance provider to determine your level of coverage.

Do I need a referral from my physician to get started?

No, a referral is not needed. You can contact me directly if you believe you or your child could benefit from therapy.

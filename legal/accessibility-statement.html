Cristina Magriñá Psychology is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, screen magnifiers, speech recognition software, or keyboard-only navigation.

What web accessibility means

An accessible site allows visitors with disabilities to browse the site with the same or a similar level of ease and enjoyment as other visitors. This can be achieved through the capabilities of the system on which the site is operating, and through assistive technologies.

Conformance status

This website has been built with the aim of meeting the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. This is a target rather than a certified audit result: the site has been developed and manually tested against these guidelines, but it has not been independently audited.

Accessibility measures taken on this site

  • The language of the site and of each page is declared in the markup.
  • Pages use a clear, logical heading structure, and the reading order matches the visual order.
  • Landmark regions (header, navigation, main content, footer) are marked up so they can be navigated directly.
  • A “Skip to content” link is the first item reachable by keyboard on every page.
  • All functionality — including the navigation menu and the expandable question panels — can be operated using a keyboard alone.
  • Visible focus indicators are shown for every interactive element.
  • Text and background colour combinations were chosen to meet the required contrast ratios.
  • Images that convey information have alternative text; decorative images are hidden from assistive technologies.
  • Motion and animation are reduced automatically for visitors whose system requests reduced motion.
  • Text can be resized and the layout reflows without loss of content or functionality.

Known limitations

The online booking system is provided by a third party. Its accessibility is outside our direct control, and we therefore declare partial compliance for that part of the booking journey. If you encounter a barrier while booking, please email us and we will arrange your appointment directly.

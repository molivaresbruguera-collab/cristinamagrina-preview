Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The privacy of visitors to this website is important to Cristina Magriñá Psychology. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through this website, how it may be used, and how it is protected.

1. Information Collected Through This Website

This website is hosted and managed through [hosting provider]. When you visit the website, [hosting provider] and technologies used on the website may automatically collect certain technical information, such as your IP address, browser and device information, approximate location, pages visited, referral information, and information about how you use the website.

The website may also use cookies and similar technologies for essential website functions, security, performance, analytics, and, where applicable, marketing. Analytics information may be used to understand how visitors find and use the website, improve its content and functionality, and evaluate marketing and search-engine optimization efforts.

[hosting provider] and other third-party services used on the website may process some of this information in accordance with their own privacy policies. The specific technologies used may change as website functionality or third-party services change.

Where required by applicable law, non-essential cookies and similar technologies will only be activated after you provide consent through the website's cookie consent mechanism. You may manage or withdraw consent through the available cookie or privacy settings.

2. Contact Me Form

If you choose to contact Cristina Magriñá through the website's Contact Me form, you may be asked to provide your name, email address and/or telephone number, as well as the content of your message.

Information submitted through the form is processed through [hosting provider] and may be stored within [hosting provider]'s systems and transmitted to Cristina Magriñá by email notification. It is used to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you regarding your request.

Please do not include sensitive, confidential, or detailed health information in your message. The Contact Me form is intended for general inquiries about psychological services and is not a secure platform for communicating sensitive clinical information.

If you subsequently engage psychological services, information required for those services will be collected and managed through separate systems and procedures as part of the client intake and consent process.

3. How Website Information Is Used

Information collected through this website may be used to:

  • operate, maintain, and secure the website;
  • understand website traffic and usage;
  • improve website content and functionality;
  • evaluate marketing and search-engine optimization;
  • respond to inquiries submitted through the Contact Me form; and
  • comply with applicable legal requirements.

Personal information collected through the website will not be sold or rented to third parties.

4. Protecting Your Information

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information collected through the website against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

However, no website, internet transmission, or email communication can be guaranteed to be completely secure. For this reason, please avoid sending sensitive or confidential information through the Contact Me form.

5. Third-Party Services

This website is hosted by [hosting provider] and may use third-party services for website functionality, security, analytics, marketing, or other purposes. These providers may process technical or usage information in connection with providing their services and have their own privacy policies.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the nature of the information collected and applicable legislation, you may have rights regarding access to or correction of personal information held about you.

If you have questions or concerns about information collected through this website, please contact:

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technology, third-party services, or applicable privacy requirements. The current version will be posted on this website.

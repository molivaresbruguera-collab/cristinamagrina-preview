1. Scope

This policy explains when a session can be cancelled or rescheduled, and when a fee is refunded. It applies to all psychological services provided by Cristina Magriñá psychological services and booked through this website.

2. Free initial consultation

The 15-minute initial consultation is free of charge. There is no fee to cancel or reschedule it, although we appreciate as much notice as you can give.

3. Cancelling or rescheduling a session

  • More than [notice period — to confirm, e.g. 24 or 48 hours] before the session: you may cancel or reschedule at no charge. Any fee already paid is refunded in full or credited to a future session, at your preference.
  • Less than [notice period — to confirm] before the session: [late-cancellation fee — to confirm: full fee / partial fee / no charge].
  • Missed sessions: where a session is not attended and no notice is given, [no-show terms — to confirm].

4. Exceptional circumstances

Illness, family emergencies, and other unforeseen events happen. Where a session is missed or cancelled late for such reasons, the cancellation fee may be waived at our discretion. Please get in touch as soon as you are able.

5. Technical problems

Sessions are held by secure video call. If a session cannot go ahead, or is significantly shortened, because of a technical failure on our side, you will not be charged; we will reschedule or refund the session in full.

6. Cancellations by the practice

If we need to cancel a session, we will offer you the earliest suitable alternative appointment or a full refund of any fee paid for that session.

7. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original method of payment within [processing time — to confirm, e.g. 10 business days]. Your bank or card provider may take additional time to show the funds.

8. Insurance

Psychological services are not covered by OHIP. Where your private insurance plan offers coverage, reimbursement is arranged between you and your insurer; a cancellation fee charged under this policy is generally not reimbursable.

9. Requesting a refund or asking a question

To cancel a session, request a refund, or ask about this policy, email info@cristinamagrina.ca. Please do not include sensitive personal or medical information in your message.

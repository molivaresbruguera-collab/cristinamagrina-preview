Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Free Initial Consultation

The 15-minute initial consultation is offered free of charge. There is no fee to cancel or reschedule this consultation, although as much notice as possible is appreciated.

Psychological Services

Fees, cancellation policies, and other terms related to ongoing psychological services are discussed at the onset of services and provided in writing in the therapy consent form before treatment begins.

Once a psychological service or session has been provided, the fee for that service is non-refundable, except where otherwise required by applicable law or professional regulations.

If you have any questions about fees, cancellations, or refunds, please contact me at info@cristinamagrina.ca. Please do not include sensitive personal or medical information in your message to protect your confidentiality.

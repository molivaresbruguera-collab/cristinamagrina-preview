All ages

Parent Support

This therapy is different from parent consultations that support a child's own therapy.

This is a space for you: your experience, your struggles, and your growth as a parent, at any stage of your child's life. Whether you are adjusting to life with a new baby, navigating the teenage years, or figuring out your relationship with an adult child, parenting brings challenges that deserve their own support.

Teen Therapy (13-17)

Supporting teens through online psychotherapy across Ontario, Quebec, and Nova Scotia.

Adolescence can come with many pressures, changes, clinical issues, and challenges characteristic of this developmental stage. I offer a safe and comfortable space to make things better.
